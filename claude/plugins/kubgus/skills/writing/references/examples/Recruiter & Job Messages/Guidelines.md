# Recruiter & Job Messages - Guidelines

Short professional messages around hiring - both inbound (replying to recruiters, being declined) and outbound (declining an offer). Usually over LinkedIn/WhatsApp/email, usually in Slovak. The point of these examples is to keep the register and strategy consistent so future ones go fast.

## Register

- **Match the register the relationship established.** Informal tykanie when the recruiter has already tykáme'd (most LinkedIn threads land here); vykanie plural under an "Ahoj" opener for a warm-but-professional team you're not on first-name terms with (see the startup decline). Don't formalize a thread that's already casual, or over-familiarize one that isn't.
- **Sounds like Kubo, not like an assistant.** The fastest tell of AI writing here is generic reassurance filler - lines like *"žiadny stres, takéto veci sa proste stávajú"* read as canned and got cut. Prefer a specific, slightly understated human beat (*"Škoda, že to nevyšlo, ale chápem."*) over a smooth platitude.
- Light, dry, never gushing. No "ďakujem za úžasnú príležitosť" energy.
- Plain hyphen `-` only. A sparing emoji (`:)`) is fine if the thread already has them.

## Strategy

- **Stay gracious, never guilt-trip.** Even when the other side ghosted or dragged (e.g. closing a position after a 4-6 h take-home and two weeks of silence), don't make them feel it. Burning a warm contact costs more than the momentary satisfaction. Grace reads as confident; complaint reads as wounded.
- **Ask for what's fair, framed around effort already invested.** The hard-to-refuse angle for feedback on an assignment is the time you put in ("nech ten čas nebol nadarmo"), not the company's behavior. Frame it as a request, not a reproach.
- **Don't draw attention to the friction.** A brief gracious acknowledgement does the work; naming the ghosting/delay explicitly just spotlights it.
- **Keep the door open** when the relationship may pay off later - mirror their own offer to stay in touch. Put the follow-up on whoever actually holds the information: if the recruiter is the one who'll know when a slot opens, invite *them* to reach out ("určite sa ozvi") rather than promising a "rád sa ozvem" you can't realistically execute.
- **When declining, give the real reason diplomatically.** Don't hide behind "took another offer" alone - name the actual tradeoff (e.g. protecting personal time over startup intensity) in a way that respects them rather than indicting their pace, and affirm their product/vision specifically so the no reads as about your life, not your doubt in them.

## Watch the grammar

Slovak subject/verb agreement on collective nouns trips up auto-drafts: `tím` is singular → `rozhodol/rozširoval`, not `rozhodli`. Proofread agreement before sending.

## Worked examples

- `Msg Life - Position Closed.md` - recruiter closes the position after a take-home; reply acknowledges gracefully, asks for constructive feedback, keeps contact open.
- `Declining a Startup Offer.md` - turning down an offer to protect personal time; honest real reason, diplomatically framed, relationship kept warm.
