# Voice Reference - Unedited Originals

Two messages **written entirely by Kubo, with no AI involvement**, preserved verbatim. Every
other file in this folder is an edited or co-written result; these are the raw input. When a
draft needs to sound like him, match these, not the polished versions.

Typos, doubled spaces and repeated phrases are left in deliberately - they mark these as
unedited, and separating a habit from a slip is the whole point (see the last section).

## The voice - what to preserve

- **Hedged volition as politeness.** "Radi by sme...", "chcel by som...", "by som rád uviedol".
  This is his default register with parents, not padding. Flattening it to "chceme" reads
  colder than he is. Keep it - except where he is announcing a decision already taken, where
  the hedge undercuts the message.
- **Long compound sentences.** Three or four clauses joined by commas, resolving at the end.
  He does not write in short declaratives, and chopping his prose into them is the fastest way
  to make an edit sound like someone else.
- **Formal connectives that carry structure.** "V prvom rade", "Zároveň", "To však", "Preto",
  "V rámci tohto týždňa". They signal where he is in the argument.
- **Explicit emotional validation.** "Oprávnenú neistotu a frustráciu", "máte právo vedieť".
  He names what parents feel and grants it standing, rather than alluding to it.
- **Forward commitments about his own conduct.** "V budúcnosti vynaložíme väčšiu snahu...",
  "si dáme záležať na tom, aby...". Nearly every apology he writes ends with one.
- **Mixed person, deliberately.** Plural for the troop ("uvedomujeme si"), singular for
  himself ("chcel by som sa ospravedlniť", "poskytnem vám"). He switches inside one message
  and it reads naturally - do not normalize it.
- **One colloquial word inside a formal frame.** "Pokope", "šum". A single informal register
  break per message keeps it from sounding institutional.
- **`_Underscore labels:_`** to head sections in WhatsApp, and a bulleted "Čo môžete očakávať"
  block for anything the reader must act on.

## Drafting artifacts - fix these, they are not the voice

- **Repeating a phrase inside one paragraph.** "Uviesť na pravú mieru" appears twice in three
  sentences of Original 1 - and means "to correct a falsehood", which is not the job it was
  doing either.
- **Slips.** "Ospravedlňujeme sa sa", "sme optimistický" (-> optimistickí), "snahu s vami na
  komunikovať", doubled spaces after sentences.
- **Abbreviations in parent-facing text.** "Šk. roka" - write it out.
- **The buried actionable block.** Original 1 puts "Čo môžete očakávať" at the very bottom,
  under two paragraphs of apology, where WhatsApp collapses it behind "Read more".

The distinction matters: an edit that removes the artifacts and keeps the habits still sounds
like him. One that tidies the habits produces generic prose he will not recognize.
