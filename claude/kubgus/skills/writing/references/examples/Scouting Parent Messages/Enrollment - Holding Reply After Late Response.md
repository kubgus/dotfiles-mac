# Enrollment - Holding Reply After Late Response

**Situation:** Email to a parent who had asked about enrolling their child and then heard
nothing for weeks, because the oddiel was mid-reorganization and could not say which družina
would have a free slot. Sent individually, so `Vy` is capitalized throughout and the
signature is appended automatically by the mail client.

**Goal:** Break the silence before the answer exists. Confirm they have not been forgotten,
explain the delay honestly, and commit to a date that can be beaten rather than missed.

## Message

> Dobrý deň,
>
> ďakujeme za Váš záujem o skauting a ospravedlňujeme sa, že sa Vám ozývame až teraz. So začiatkom školského roka u nás v oddiele prebieha reorganizácia družín a trvalo nám dlhšie, kým sme Vám vedeli povedať niečo konkrétne. Vieme, že to u Vás vyvolalo zbytočnú neistotu, a mrzí nás to.
>
> Váš záujem každopádne evidujeme. Tento víkend zasadá oddielová rada a rozdelí členov do družín - najneskôr začiatkom budúceho týždňa Vám potom pošleme konkrétne informácie o voľnom mieste a o termínoch družinoviek.
>
> Ďakujeme za Vašu doterajšiu trpezlivosť.
>
> Prajem pekný deň,

## Why it works

- **Opens by thanking them for the interest.** These are people who want in; an administrative
  notice wastes that. Every enrollment reply in `System Prompt.md` opens the same way.
- **"Voľné miesto", never "prijatie".** A parent who has waited weeks reads "informácie o
  prijatí" as "your child might be rejected" - the opposite of what a holding reply is for.
  "Voľné miesto" says the same thing honestly without putting the child on trial.
- **The delay gets a real cause.** "Prebieha reorganizácia družín" turns an unexplained
  failure into a constraint. Vague apology without a reason reads as evasion.
- **No hedging on the harm.** "Vieme, že to u Vás vyvolalo zbytočnú neistotu" states it as
  fact. A hedge ("chaos, ktorý naša neskorá odpoveď *mohla* spôsobiť") reads as reluctance
  to own it.
- **Promises a floor, not a ceiling.** "Najneskôr začiatkom budúceho týždňa" can be beaten by
  sending on Sunday; "už túto nedeľu" can only be missed. With someone you have already kept
  waiting, a second missed deadline costs far more than the first.
- **Keeps a concrete anchor.** "Tento víkend zasadá oddielová rada" proves something is
  actually moving, even though the delivery date is deliberately soft.
- **Stays short.** Four short paragraphs. A holding reply that runs long looks like it is
  padding around the absent answer.

## Variant: the reply that isn't late

Most inquiries get answered in time. The same constraint still applies - the rada hasn't sat,
so no slot can be named - but the apology has nothing to attach to, and apologising anyway
invents a failure the parent hadn't noticed.

> Dobrý deň,
>
> ďakujeme za Váš záujem o skauting, veľmi nás teší, že by ste sa k nám radi pridali.
>
> So začiatkom školského roka u nás v oddiele prebieha reorganizácia družín, takže Vám ešte nevieme povedať, v ktorej z nich máme voľné miesto. Tento víkend zasadá oddielová rada a rozdelí členov do družín - najneskôr začiatkom budúceho týždňa Vám potom pošleme konkrétne informácie o voľnom mieste a o termínoch družinoviek.
>
> Ak by ste sa medzitým chceli na čokoľvek opýtať, pokojne mi napíšte.
>
> Prajem pekný deň,

**What changes, and why:**

- **The apology and the "vyvolalo neistotu" sentence are gone.** Nothing was owed. Apologising
  on time reads as anxious, and it tells a parent there was a problem before they suspected one.
- **The constraint becomes the subject.** In the late version the reorganization explains the
  silence; here it explains why the answer isn't in this email. Same fact, different job -
  which is why the paragraph is rebuilt rather than trimmed.
- **The open door is added, not kept.** "Pokojne mi napíšte" belongs only here. In the late
  version, inviting more correspondence from someone still waiting on the first answer asks
  them to do the chasing.
- **Everything load-bearing survives:** thanks first, "voľné miesto" not "prijatie", a floor
  that can be beaten, and the rada as a concrete anchor proving something is moving.

Rule of thumb: **apologise for the delay, not for the constraint.** A holding reply sent on
time is not a failure - it's just an honest answer that the answer isn't ready.

## Writing one for a named family

A personalised version is the generic variant plus the answers to what *this* parent actually
asked - and nothing else. Two traps, both from a real draft:

- **Do not hand back what they already told you.** A parent who wrote "bývame na [CLUBROOM_LOCATION], tak ak
  by ste mali voľné kapacity tam" does not need "klubovňu máte naozaj blízko - stretávame sa
  na [CLUBROOM_LOCATION]." It answers a question she answered herself, staged as good news, in a
  one-line paragraph built to feel like a win. Her real question was *capacity* on [CLUBROOM_LOCATION], and
  that belongs as a clause inside the paragraph already discussing it - "v ktorej z družín na
  [CLUBROOM_LOCATION] máme pre Jožka voľné miesto" - not as a beat of its own. The tell is the intensifier
  pile-up that props such a sentence up: "našťastie", "naozaj", two in nine words.
- **Silence about a child is an answer about that child.** If a parent names three children and
  the reply handles one, the other two read as refused. One sentence fixes it - but it has to
  name who picks them up ("ohľadom Kristínky a Katarínky sa Vám ozve iný oddiel"), not narrate
  our intentions about them. An earlier draft said we would write separately because we wanted
  to "vyriešiť to poriadne, nie jednou vetou": a sentence about our own diligence, which tells
  the parent nothing she can act on and delays the one fact she needs.
