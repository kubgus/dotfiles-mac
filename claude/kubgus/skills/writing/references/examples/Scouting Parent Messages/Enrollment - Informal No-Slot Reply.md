# Enrollment - Informal No-Slot Reply

**Situation:** Email to a father who is himself a scout and whose older son is already in
another oddiel. He sent an enrollment for his younger son ([DIEŤA]) in July, writing
`Ahojte` and signing `so skautským pozdravom`. The troop answered in September with the
formal holding reply in `Enrollment - Holding Reply After Late Response.md`, promised
details within a week, and then went silent for two months. He sent a one-line reminder in
November. The real answer was that no družina in the oddiel had a free slot for the boy's
age.

**Origin:** Written by Kubo. He reviewed an edited version and kept his own draft; only the
closing `Vďaka za trpezlivosť.` came from the edit. Treat it as a voice reference for
informal email.

## Message

> Ahoj,
>
> prepáč za dlhé ticho, trochu som sa v tom zasekol lebo [DIEŤA] nemáme v [ODDIEL] úplne kam zaradiť.
>
> Čo som teraz urobil:
> Zapísal som [DIEŤA] do čakacej listiny, aby sme ho mali zaevidovaného, keby sa otvorí družina v jeho veku
> Overil som kapacity existujúcich družín v [ODDIEL] a iných oddieloch a komunikoval som s nimi váš záujem, keby sa nejaké miesto uvoľnilo
>
> Budeme sa snažiť to čo najproaktívnejšie riešiť. V januári sa dosť pravdepodobne otvorí nový skautský oddiel, tak sa môžme spojiť vtedy a znova sa na to pozrieť. Môžeš mi písať na WhatsApp: [TELEFÓN] (asi ma už aj máš).
>
> Vďaka za trpezlivosť.
>
> S pozdravom,

## What makes it his

- **Register mirrors the parent, not the genre.** The father wrote `Ahojte` from the first
  message, so the reply uses `Ahoj` and tykanie. The earlier `Dobrý deň` with vykanie was the
  mismatch. `váš záujem` stays lowercase plural: it means the family, not a polite `Vy`.
- **The apology is one clause, and the cause follows it.** "Prepáč za dlhé ticho, trochu som
  sa v tom zasekol lebo..." admits the stall in his own words instead of a formal apology
  paragraph.
- **Bare action list.** `Čo som teraz urobil:` with plain lines, no bullets or punctuation at
  the line ends. In a short personal email that reads as a person typing, not a report.
- **Colloquial grammar stays.** `keby sa otvorí` (standard: `keby sa otvorila`) and `môžme`
  are how he talks. In informal mail they are a language quirk, not a slip - do not correct
  them. In formal messages to parents, the standard forms apply.
- **Softeners that are honest.** "Nemáme úplne kam zaradiť", "dosť pravdepodobne", "asi ma už
  aj máš" - each one reflects real uncertainty, so they stay.

## What he chose not to take

The edit he declined led with the no-slot news as its own sentence, named the missed
September promise, replaced "Budeme sa snažiť to čo najproaktívnejšie riešiť" with a dated
commitment to write in January, and swapped "kapacity" and "komunikoval som s nimi" for
plainer words. With an informal correspondent he kept the softer, less structured version.
Offer these as suggestions in this register, not as corrections.
