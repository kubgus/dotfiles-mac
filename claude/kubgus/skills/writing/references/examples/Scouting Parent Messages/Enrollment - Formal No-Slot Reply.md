# Enrollment - Formal No-Slot Reply

**Situation:** Email to a parent who asked, "zatiaľ orientačne", whether the troop had a
place for her 14-year-old son. She wrote `Dobrý deň` with vykanie and mentioned which part
of town they live in and that the clubroom is close. The answer was that no družina had a
free slot, and the boy went on the waiting list. Sent individually, so `Vy` is capitalized
and the mail client appends the signature.

## Message

> Dobrý deň,
>
> ďakujeme za Váš záujem o skauting. V našom oddiele však momentálne nemáme pre Vášho syna voľné miesto, pretože naše družiny sú plne obsadené.
>
> Zapísali sme ho preto na čakaciu listinu. Ozveme sa Vám, hneď ako sa v niektorej z družín uvoľní miesto alebo ako otvoríme novú družinu.
>
> Ďakujeme za pochopenie.
>
> Prajem pekný deň,

## Why it works

- **Register mirrors the parent.** She wrote formally, so the reply stays formal. Compare
  `Enrollment - Informal No-Slot Reply.md`, where the parent wrote `Ahojte` and got tykanie.
- **Thanks first, then the no in the second sentence.** The refusal is not delayed behind
  reassurance, and it carries its reason in the same sentence.
- **Her location is not handed back.** She said where they live; repeating it ("klubovňu
  máte blízko") answers a question she did not ask.
- **The last paragraph of substance is the commitment.** What happens next - the waiting
  list and the two triggers for contact - is the fact she keeps.
- **Nothing invented to soften it.** No other oddiel and no date for a new družina, because
  neither was known. If a real option exists, it goes in one sentence after the waiting list.
