# Declining a Startup Offer

**Situation:** A startup made Kubo an offer. He declined - he took another offer and, more honestly, wanted to protect his personal time and a calmer lifestyle rather than commit to startup intensity.

**Goal:** Turn it down without burning the relationship - honest about the real reason, diplomatic in framing, door left open.

## Message (sent)

> Ahoj, ešte raz vám veľmi pekne ďakujem za ponuku a čas, ktorý ste mi venovali. Po dôkladnom zvážení mojej situácie som sa nakoniec rozhodol vziať inú ponuku. V tejto fáze môjho života nedokážem zaručiť také časové nasadenie, aby som vedel fungovať v startupe a zároveň si zachoval svoje iné záväzky voči sebe aj iným ľuďom. Naďalej fandím vášmu produktu a vízii a verím, že sa vám bude dariť. Rád s vami zostanem v kontakte a budem sledovať váš ďalší rast :)

## Why it works

- Opens and closes with genuine thanks for their time - the cost they sunk into him.
- States the decision plainly, then gives the *real* reason framed diplomatically: not "I don't want startup hours" but "v tejto fáze môjho života nedokážem zaručiť také časové nasadenie." Honest, not an excuse, not insulting their pace.
- Names the time-vs-personal-commitments tradeoff openly rather than hiding behind "another opportunity" alone - respects them with the truth.
- Affirms their product and vision specifically, so the decline reads as about his life, not about doubting them.
- Keeps the door open warmly.

## Register note

Unlike the recruiter-thread example, this addresses a team in **vykanie plural** ("ďakujem vám", "venovali ste mi") under an informal "Ahoj" opener - the standard Slovak register for a warm-but-professional group you're not on tykanie terms with. Match whatever the relationship established.
