# Msg Life - Position Closed After Take-Home

**Situation:** Applied via LinkedIn (recruiter, informal tykanie). Did a Teams intro, then a take-home GitHub assignment (~4-6 h). Recruiter went quiet ~2 weeks, then closed the position - colleagues had already picked a candidate - and asked to stay in touch for future openings.

**Goal of the reply:** (1) acknowledge gracefully, (2) ask for fair constructive feedback on the assignment, (3) keep the door open. No guilt-tripping over the silence.

## Inbound (recruiter)

> Ahoj Jakub, bol som na dovolenke. Za ten krátky čas tam kolegovia našli jedného uchádzača, takže sa mi pozícia uzatvára, ale ostaňme prosím v kontakte ak by náhodou sa rozhodli doplniť team. Dakujem Jakub

## Reply (sent)

> Ahoj, ďakujem za info. Škoda, že to nevyšlo, ale chápem. Držím kolegom palce s novým človekom.
>
> Keďže som do zadania investoval slušný kus času, ocenil by som, ak by mi teda kolega vedel poslať aspoň krátky konštruktívny feedback na to, čo som odovzdal - čo bolo fajn a kde mám priestor zlepšiť sa. Pomohlo by mi to posunúť sa, nech ten čas nebol nadarmo :)
>
> A jasné, ostaňme v kontakte. Ak by sa tím v budúcnosti rozširoval, určite sa ozvi.
>
> Pekný deň a ešte raz vďaka!

## Why it works

- Opening beat is specific and human (*"Škoda, že to nevyšlo, ale chápem"*), not platitude filler.
- Feedback ask is anchored to invested time, framed as a request - hard to refuse, easy to grant.
- The two-week silence is never mentioned; grace over grievance.
- Closing mirrors the recruiter's own "stay in touch" offer, and puts the follow-up on him (*"určite sa ozvi"*) since he's the one who'll know when a slot opens - not a follow-up Kubo could realistically execute himself.
